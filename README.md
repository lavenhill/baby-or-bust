# 少子女化拯救器 Baby or Bust
A swipe-card policy game about Taiwan's low birth rate. You are the Premier from 2018 onward: every card is a real policy dilemma, and every choice spends money, patience, or both.
一款關於台灣少子女化政策的滑卡遊戲。你是 2018 年起的行政院長，每張卡都是真實發生過的政策兩難。
Play / 開始玩：https://lavenhill.github.io/baby-or-bust/play.html
• Inspired by the card-swiping mechanic of Reigns; all art, writing, and characters are original.
• Every card ends with what Taiwan's government actually chose, with sources.
• Built for the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon 2026.
